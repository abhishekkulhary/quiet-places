Your phone goes quiet where it should.

Save the places where your phone should hush — a cinema, an office, a bedroom. Quiet Places switches to silent, vibrate or Do Not Disturb when you arrive, and puts your ringer back exactly as you had it when you leave.

01 Built for the way phones actually work

Restores what you had

It records your ringer state before changing anything, and gives exactly that back when you leave — not a blanket "normal". Change it yourself while you're there and it stops managing it. It won't fight you.

Always know where you stand

Every place shows the distance to its centre and which side of the boundary you're on — "5 m · inside by 95 m". When GPS is too poor to be sure, it says so.

Draw it on a real map

Drag the pin, drag the edge to resize, long-press to drop a new one. Overlapping places are flagged, with the setting that wins.

A fraction of a percent of battery

Uses the operating system's own geofencing instead of polling your location. Survives a reboot and keeps working with the app closed.

02 Offline by default

Your places, their names and your history never leave your phone. No account, no analytics, no crash reporting. Maps are opt-in — until you turn them on the app makes no network requests at all.

  • Location, all the time — geofence transitions arrive while the app is closed; that is the whole feature.
  • Do Not Disturb access — Android requires it to change silent and DND modes.
  • Notifications — to tell you the moment your ringer changed.
  • Internet — map tiles only, and only if you turn maps on.

03 Get Quiet Places

Download the APK from the latest GitHub release and open it on your phone (you may need to allow installing from your browser). Updates install over the previous version.

Download for Android

iPhone? Apple provides no way for an app to change your ringer, so there is no iOS version. A Shortcuts Focus automation is the closest hands-free equivalent.
